A subscription is a recurring payment for a service, such as an OTT platform, music service, or utility, that renews automatically unless cancelled.
An EMI is a fixed monthly installment paid toward a larger one-time purchase, such as electronics or a vehicle.
The "next due date" refers to the date on which the next payment for a subscription or EMI installment is expected.
"Auto-renew" means a subscription will automatically continue and charge the user unless they manually cancel it.
A payment method refers to how a bill is being paid, such as UPI, card, or netbanking — it does not process payments itself, only records how something is paid.
Payment history is a permanent record of past payments and cannot be edited once created.
