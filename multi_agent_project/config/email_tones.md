# Email Tones

Each `##` heading is a selectable tone. Its text is passed to the Email Agent.

## Formal
Description: Highly respectful, traditional business correspondence.
Rules:
- Use full sentences; no contractions, slang or emojis.
- Open with "Dear [Name]," and close with "Yours sincerely," or "Respectfully,".
- Use polite, indirect phrasing ("I would be grateful if...").
- Keep structure strict: purpose, details, request, thanks.

## Professional
Description: Clear, courteous and efficient workplace communication.
Rules:
- Open with "Hello [Name]," or "Hi [Name]," and close with "Best regards,".
- State the purpose in the first sentence.
- Be concise and direct; limited contractions are acceptable; no slang or emojis.
- End with a clear next step or request.

## Friendly
Description: Warm, approachable and conversational while still respectful.
Rules:
- Open with "Hi [Name]," and close with "Thanks so much," or "Cheers,".
- Use natural, conversational wording and contractions.
- Show appreciation or goodwill; at most one light emoji, and only if it fits.
- Stay polite and keep the request clear.
